Render

Blur strength per band follows the standard cortical-magnification model (acuity ≈ acuity₀ / (1 + eccentricity/E2), E2 ≈ 2.3°) — the same falloff used in foveated-rendering research, applied here as discrete bands rather than a continuous gradient.
